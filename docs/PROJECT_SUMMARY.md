# Project Summary

## Product

"Домашний диспетчер еды" is a family food planning app for Pavel's family. The MVP helps decide what to cook, what is left, what is in the freezer or pantry, and what must be bought.

The app should feel like a living mobile kitchen assistant: warm, useful, calm, and pleasant to open often.

## Current Stack

- Next.js App Router
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS

## User Preferences Captured

- The user preferred the warm Vercel version over later cyberpunk/mobile redesign experiments.
- Avoid random food photos. Images must match the product or dish.
- The app should allow editing family member data, food preferences, dislikes, restrictions, and notes.
- The user wants future input by voice: "надиктовать что я люблю кушать".
- The user wants future photo input: upload a dish/photo and say "добавь в мои любимые блюда".
- The app should make realistic meal plans for a Belarusian family with medium-high income.
- Breakfasts must be realistic. Sweet pastry, jam, and cocoa should not accidentally become a strange complete breakfast.
- Shopping list visibility must be obvious and easy to find.
